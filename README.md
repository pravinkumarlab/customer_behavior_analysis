# 🛒 Customer Behavior Analysis  
**SQL & Python | Marketing & Customer Analytics**

## 📌 Project Overview
ShopEasy, an online retail business, is facing declining customer engagement and conversion rates despite increased marketing investments.  
This project applies a **data-driven approach using SQL and Python** to analyze customer behavior, reviews, engagement, and journey patterns in order to generate actionable business insights.

All analytical insights are derived **purely from SQL queries**, while Python is used for **data cleaning, database setup, and automation**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
ShopEasy is experiencing:
- Reduced customer interactions
- Declining conversion rates
- High marketing spend with low ROI
- Limited understanding of customer feedback

---

## 🧠 Skills & Tools Used
- SQL (Joins, Aggregations, Subqueries, CTEs)
- Python (pandas, mysql-connector-python)
- MySQL Database
- CSV Data Cleaning & Processing

🚫 SQLAlchemy is **not used**

---

## 🏢 Domain
Marketing & Customer Analytics

## 🗂 Dataset Description

- customer_reviews.csv – Product reviews and ratings
- customers.csv – Customer demographics
- products.csv – Product details
- geography.csv – Customer locations

## 📊 Key Insights
- Checkout stage has the highest drop-off
- Product pages have high engagement but low conversion
- Repeat customer rate is low
- Product performance varies by region

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Simplify checkout process
- Improve product page clarity
- Address negative review themes
- Introduce loyalty programs
